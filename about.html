Our Story

From a Local Corner to Your Favorite Spot

Wahad Shay began as a small vision to bring the soulful, high-energy cafeteria culture of the UAE to the digital age. We believed that great food doesn't need to be formal—it needs to be fresh, flavorful, and shared with friends. Our journey started with a single secret recipe for Karak tea and has grown into a destination for snack lovers across the city.

Our Mission

To serve joy, one bite at a time.

Our mission is simple: to provide a high-octane sensory experience through authentic snacks that celebrate UAE heritage. We obsess over the details—the crunch of the paratha, the temperature of the tea, and the smile on your face. We are here to fuel your day and your late-night cravings with uncompromising quality and approachable friendliness.
